## 🎨 LOGO INVENTORY

### **1. PRAVYOM (Main Brand)**
**Visual Elements:**
- **Infinity symbol (∞)** in gold-to-white gradient
- **Network nodes** on top (5 connected dots)
- **U-shaped base** in white (container/foundation)
- **Dark navy background** (#0A1628 approximately)
- **Typography:** Bold, modern sans-serif "PARVYOM"

**Symbolism:**
- **Infinity:** Endless knowledge, eternal growth
- **Network nodes:** Distributed system, connectivity
- **U-shape:** Container (like Sanskrit "उ"), foundation, support
- **Gold gradient:** Transformation (dark → light), Venus energy
- **Navy background:** Saturn stability, depth, cosmos

**Philosophy Alignment:**
- ✅ **Suśruta:** U-shape = body container, nodes = nerve network
- ✅ **Pāṇini:** Modular elements (∞ + nodes + U) = generative grammar
- ✅ **Śukra Nīti:** Gold gradient = beauty with purpose
- ✅ **Awareness Flow:** Dark → Light (Tamas → Sattva)

## ✅ LOGO DESIGN PRINCIPLES (For Future Logos)

**Must Have:**
1. **Navy background** (#0A1628) - Saturn stability
2. **Gold accent** (#E8B44F) - Venus transformation
3. **Simple geometry** - Pāṇinian minimalism
4. **Symbolic meaning** - Connects to philosophy
5. **Scalable** - Works at 16px to 1000px
6. **Memorable** - Unique, recognizable

**Must Avoid:**
1. ❌ Complex gradients (hard to reproduce)
2. ❌ Too many colors (breaks unity)
3. ❌ Thin lines (doesn't scale)
4. ❌ Generic icons (no meaning)
5. ❌ Trendy effects (ages poorly)
