### **Responsive Behavior**

**Desktop (>992px):**
- Full sidebar always visible
- Collapsible with toggle button
- User profile in sidebar

**Tablet (768px - 992px):**
- Sidebar auto-collapses to 80px
- Expands on hover
- Icons only when collapsed

**Mobile (<768px):**
- Sidebar hidden by default
- Hamburger menu to open
- Full overlay when open
- User avatar in top header

### **Interactions**
- **Hover:** Menu items highlight with gold
- **Selected:** Active page has gold border + gradient background
- **Dropdown:** Click to expand/collapse groups
- **User Menu:** Click profile for settings/logout

## 🎯 HOW TO USE

### **Access the Dashboard:**
1. Go to: `http://localhost:5173/basic-dashboard`
2. You'll see the new sidebar on the left
3. Click any menu item to navigate

### **Toggle Sidebar:**
- Click the hamburger icon (☰) in top-left
- Sidebar collapses to icons only
- Click again to expand

### **Navigate:**
- Click main items (Dashboard, Wallet, etc.)
- Click dropdown arrows to expand groups
- Click sub-items to navigate to pages

### **User Menu:**
- Click your profile at bottom of sidebar
- Options: Profile Settings, Security, Logout
