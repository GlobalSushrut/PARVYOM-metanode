# ENTERPRISE PAGE PSYCHOLOGICAL ARCHITECTURE
## 7-Log Rule + 5 Hierarchy + 13 Compartments

**Date**: 2025-10-31  
**Purpose**: Redesign Enterprise/Contribution page with psychological clarity that drives contribution through vision and value, not emotion  
**Principle**: People contribute when they see clear value, catch the wave early, and understand the hierarchy of opportunity

---

## 🎯 PSYCHOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK

### **7-Log Rule (7 Major Compartments)**
Human brain processes information in chunks of 7±2. We use exactly 7 major sections:

1. **Generic Introduction** - What this is (clarity)
2. **Contributors** - Individual developers/researchers
3. **Pilot Partners** - Testing/validation partners
4. **Institutional Pilots** - Enterprises/governments
5. **Pre-Fundable Pilots** - Early-stage funding partners
6. **IP Investor Pilots** - Strategic IP/technology investors
7. **Web3 Decentralized Vision** - Community/ecosystem builders

### **5 Hierarchy Levels (Within Each Compartment)**
Each section follows a 5-level psychological progression:

1. **What** - Clear definition (no ambiguity)
2. **Why Now** - Timing/wave (FOMO, but logical)
3. **What You Get** - Concrete value (not promises)
4. **What's Required** - Honest requirements (no surprises)
5. **How to Start** - Clear next step (no friction)

### **13 Sub-Compartments (Distributed Across 7 Sections)**
Total of 13 distinct contribution types across the 7 major sections:

**Section 1: Generic (1 compartment)**
- Overview

**Section 2: Contributors (2 compartments)**
- Code contributors
- Research contributors

**Section 3: Pilot Partners (2 compartments)**
- Technical pilots
- Business pilots

**Section 4: Institutional Pilots (2 compartments)**
- Enterprise pilots
- Government pilots

**Section 5: Pre-Fundable Pilots (2 compartments)**
- Angel/seed investors
- Strategic partners

**Section 6: IP Investor Pilots (2 compartments)**
- Technology IP investors
- Patent/licensing partners

**Section 7: Web3 Vision (2 compartments)**

## 🧠 PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGERS (NOT MANIPULATION)

### **Clarity Trigger**
- Every section answers: What is this? (no ambiguity)
- Effect: Trust through transparency

### **Timing Trigger**
- Every section answers: Why now? (not "why ever")
- Effect: Urgency through logic (not FOMO)

### **Value Trigger**
- Every section answers: What do I get? (concrete, not vague)
- Effect: Motivation through self-interest

### **Honesty Trigger**
- Every section answers: What's required? (no hidden costs)
- Effect: Commitment through informed decision

### **Action Trigger**
- Every section answers: How do I start? (clear next step)
- Effect: Conversion through simplicity

## 📊 SUCCESS METRICS

**Clarity Score**: Can user identify their category in <30 seconds?  
**Value Score**: Can user articulate "what they get" after reading?  
**Action Score**: Does user know exact next step?  

**Target**: 90%+ on all three scores

1. **Remove all emotional manipulation**
   - No "limited time offers"
   - No "exclusive opportunity"
   - No "revolutionary" or "game-changing"

2. **Add all logical clarity**
   - Exact numbers (75% complete, 15 services, etc.)
   - Exact requirements (investment amounts, time commitments)
   - Exact next steps (links, emails, forms)

3. **Progressive disclosure**
   - Generic section visible to all
   - User selects category
   - Deep dive into 5-level hierarchy
   - Call-to-action appears

4. **Visual hierarchy**
   - 7 major sections clearly separated
   - 13 compartments visually distinct
   - 5 levels within each compartment
   - Clear navigation between sections
